I'm a curator, artist and researcher specialised in mentoring projects and producing exhibitions and mediations. In my practice I blend institutional critique with pop imaginaries and internet culture.

I trained in art and design, curating and artistic research. I've carried out artistic and curatorial projects in spaces such as La Capella, Santa Mònica, Hangar, Fundació Brossa, Lo Pardal, Fundació Miró, C.C. Pere Pruna or Can Basté. I'm also a member of the artist-and-curator collective Grup d'Estudi.

I'm currently running the project Unfunny_Curator, a curatorial alter ego and mediation project that reflects on various issues of the art world, blending physical and digital spaces.
